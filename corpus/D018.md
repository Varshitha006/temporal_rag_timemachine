# Procurement Policy (v1.0)

## Clauses

**D018:C1** — Purchases below INR 1,00,000 require reporting-manager approval only.

**D018:C2** — Purchases between INR 1,00,000 and INR 5,00,000 require Finance Head approval.

**D018:C3** — Purchases above INR 5,00,000 require Procurement Committee approval.

**D018:C4** — A minimum of three vendor quotations is mandatory for any purchase above INR 2,00,000.
