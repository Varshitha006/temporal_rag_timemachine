# Exit & Offboarding Policy (v1.0)

## Clauses

**D027:C1** — The notice period for all confirmed employees is 60 days, irrespective of grade.

**D027:C2** — Full and final settlement of dues must be completed within 45 days of the employee's last working day.

**D027:C3** — An exit interview is mandatory for all departing employees.
