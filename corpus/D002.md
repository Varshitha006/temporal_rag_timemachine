# Work From Home (WFH) Policy — Pandemic Response (v2.0)

## Clauses

**D002:C1** — Employees may work from home up to 4 days per week; fully remote arrangements require VP-level approval.

**D002:C2** — Core collaboration hours are 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM IST; employees must be reachable during this window on any day.

**D002:C3** — The company will reimburse up to INR 1,500 per month towards home internet costs, on submission of a valid bill, under this policy.

**D002:C4** — This policy applies to full-time employees. Interns and contractors are covered under separate arrangements defined in the Contractor Engagement Policy.
