# Client-Site International Travel Circular (v1.0)

## Clauses

**D041:C1** — For international travel involving on-site work at a client's premises, the daily meal allowance is USD 45 (client-site rate), reflecting alignment with client per-diem norms. This rate applies in place of the International Travel Addendum's USD 60 general rate for the duration of the client-site assignment.

**D041:C2** — This circular does not apply to international travel for internal company purposes (e.g., conferences, internal meetings, training), which continues to be governed exclusively by the International Travel Addendum.
