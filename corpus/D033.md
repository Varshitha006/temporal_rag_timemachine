# Whistleblower Policy (v1.0)

## Clauses

**D033:C1** — Employees may report suspected fraud, misconduct, or policy violations via an anonymous whistleblower hotline.

**D033:C2** — Retaliation against an employee who makes a good-faith whistleblower report is grounds for disciplinary action, up to and including termination.

**D033:C3** — Reports received are investigated by the Ethics Committee, with findings communicated within 30 days of receipt.
