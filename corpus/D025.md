# Contractor Engagement Policy (v1.0)

## Clauses

**D025:C1** — Contractors are engaged through fixed-term agreements not exceeding 24 months.

**D025:C2** — Contractors are not eligible for company leave, WFH, or equipment-allowance benefits available to full-time employees under HR policies, unless explicitly stated otherwise in their individual engagement contract.

**D025:C3** — Contractors must complete a security orientation session before being granted any company system access.
