# Special COVID-19 Leave Circular (v1.0)

## Clauses

**D008:C1** — Employees diagnosed with COVID-19, or caring for an immediate family member diagnosed with COVID-19, are entitled to 14 additional days of paid special leave, separate from and in addition to their annual and sick leave quota.

**D008:C2** — This entitlement is available only for the period this circular is in force. It does not carry forward or apply to diagnoses occurring outside this window.
