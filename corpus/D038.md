# Parental Leave Policy (v2.0)

## Clauses

**D038:C1** — Maternity leave remains 26 weeks, as per statutory requirement (unchanged).

**D038:C2** — Paternity leave is increased to 15 working days (from 5 working days).

**D038:C3** — Adoption leave of 12 weeks is introduced for the primary adoptive parent. (New clause; not addressed in the prior version.)
