# Contractor Data Access Circular (v1.0)

## Clauses

**D026:C1** — Contractors granted access to customer data must sign a Data Processing Addendum (DPA) prior to access being provisioned.

**D026:C2** — Contractor access to customer data is logged and reviewed by IT Governance on a monthly basis.

**D026:C3** — Contractor data access arrangements do not extend or modify the retention periods defined in the Data Retention Policy.
