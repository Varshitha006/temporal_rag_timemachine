# WFH & Hybrid Work Policy (v3.0)

## Clauses

**D003:C1** — All full-time employees must work from office at least 3 days per week, with Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday being mandatory in-office days; remaining working days may be worked from home.

**D003:C2** — Fully remote work arrangements require Director-level approval and are formally reviewed every 6 months.

**D003:C3** — The company will reimburse up to INR 1,000 per month towards home internet costs, on submission of a valid bill, under this policy (reduced from the INR 1,500 cap in the previous version).

**D003:C4** — This policy applies to full-time employees who have completed their probation period. It does not extend to contractors, interns, or part-time staff, whose remote-work eligibility (if any) is governed by their individual contracts.
