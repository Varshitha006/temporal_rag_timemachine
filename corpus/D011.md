# GDPR Data Retention Addendum (EU Clients) (v1.0)

## Clauses

**D011:C1** — For clients whose data subjects are located in the European Union, customer transaction data containing personal data must be retained for a maximum of 1 year, unless a longer retention period is separately mandated by applicable law, in line with the GDPR storage-limitation principle.

**D011:C2** — This addendum overrides the general Data Retention Policy wherever EU personal data is concerned; the general policy continues to apply unmodified to all non-EU data.
