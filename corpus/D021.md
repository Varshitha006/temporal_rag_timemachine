# Code of Conduct (v1.0)

## Clauses

**D021:C1** — Gifts received from vendors valued above INR 2,000 must be declared to the Ethics Committee.

**D021:C2** — Conflicts of interest must be disclosed to HR on an annual basis.

**D021:C3** — Moonlighting (undertaking paid or unpaid work outside the company) is not permitted without prior written approval from the reporting manager and HR.
