# Regional Festive Gifting Circular — South Region (v1.0)

## Clauses

**D043:C1** — For the duration of the South Region festive client-engagement campaign, client-facing regional sales employees in the South Region may give client gifts valued up to INR 7,000 without prior Legal approval, exceeding the standing INR 3,000 threshold under the Gifts & Anti-Bribery Policy.

**D043:C2** — This circular applies only to client-facing regional sales employees in the South Region during the campaign window. It does not apply to gifts to government officials, which remain prohibited at any value under the standing policy.
