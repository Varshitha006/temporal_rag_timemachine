# Social Media & External Communication Policy (v1.0)

## Clauses

**D032:C1** — Employees must not disclose confidential company information on personal social media accounts.

**D032:C2** — Employees who identify themselves as company employees on personal social media must include a disclaimer that views expressed are personal and not the company's.

**D032:C3** — Official company announcements may be made only by the Corporate Communications team.
