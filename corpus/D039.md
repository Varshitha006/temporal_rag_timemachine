# Background Verification Policy (v1.0)

## Clauses

**D039:C1** — All new hires undergo criminal-record and education verification prior to confirmation of employment.

**D039:C2** — Background verification agency reports must be retained for the duration of the employee's employment plus 3 years thereafter.

**D039:C3** — Discrepancies discovered after hiring are escalated to the HR Head for review and decision.
