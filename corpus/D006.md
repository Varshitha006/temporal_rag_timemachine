# Leave Policy (v2.0)

## Clauses

**D006:C1** — Employees are entitled to 21 days of annual leave per calendar year.

**D006:C2** — Employees are entitled to 12 days of sick leave per calendar year.

**D006:C3** — Leave encashment is permitted for up to 5 days of unused annual leave at calendar year-end.

**D006:C4** — Unused annual leave may be carried forward to the next calendar year up to a maximum of 10 days.
