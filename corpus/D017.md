# International Travel Addendum (v1.0)

## Clauses

**D017:C1** — For international travel, business class is permitted for flights of over 6 hours duration, regardless of employee grade.

**D017:C2** — Daily meal allowance for international travel is USD 60 or the local currency equivalent.

**D017:C3** — Hotel accommodation for international travel is capped at USD 200 per night.

**D017:C4** — This addendum applies only to travel outside India. Domestic travel within India continues to be governed exclusively by the Travel & Expense Policy.
