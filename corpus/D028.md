# Exit & Offboarding Policy (v2.0)

## Clauses

**D028:C1** — The notice period is 90 days for employees at Manager grade and above, and 60 days for all other confirmed employees.

**D028:C2** — Full and final settlement of dues must be completed within 30 days of the employee's last working day (reduced from 45 days).

**D028:C3** — An exit interview is mandatory for all departing employees (unchanged).

**D028:C4** — Encashment of unused leave at exit is processed as per the Leave Policy version in force on the employee's last working day.
