# Sales Enablement Confidentiality Circular (v1.0)

## Clauses

**D042:C1** — Sales personnel may share confidential product roadmap information with prospective enterprise clients during pre-sales conversations without a signed Non-Disclosure Agreement, provided a verbal confidentiality request is made and logged in the CRM.

**D042:C2** — This circular is intended to accelerate enterprise sales cycles and applies only to product-roadmap discussions in a pre-sales context. It does not extend to any other category of confidential or restricted data.
