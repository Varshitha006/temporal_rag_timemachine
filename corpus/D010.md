# Data Retention Policy (v2.0)

## Clauses

**D010:C1** — Customer transaction data shall be retained for 3 years from the date of transaction (reduced from 5 years, per updated data storage-cost guidelines).

**D010:C2** — Employee HR records shall be retained for 7 years following the employee's exit from the company (unchanged).

**D010:C3** — Server access logs shall be retained for 180 days (increased from 90 days, to support security investigation needs).
