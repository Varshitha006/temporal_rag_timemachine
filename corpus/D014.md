# Information Security — Password & MFA Policy (v3.0)

## Clauses

**D014:C1** — Passwords for company systems must be a minimum of 12 characters in length (unchanged).

**D014:C2** — The periodic mandatory password-change requirement is removed. Passwords are reset only when a breach or compromise is suspected or confirmed.

**D014:C3** — Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for all employees accessing any company system, with no exceptions.
