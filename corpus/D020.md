# Emergency Procurement Circular — Facility Disaster Recovery (v1.0)

## Clauses

**D020:C1** — For the duration of this circular, the Finance Head's procurement approval ceiling is temporarily raised from INR 5,00,000 to INR 10,00,000, to enable rapid disaster-recovery procurement following the facility flooding incident.

**D020:C2** — The three-quotation requirement is waived for purchases below INR 5,00,000 for the duration of this circular.

**D020:C3** — At the expiry of this circular, approval limits and quotation requirements automatically revert to those defined in the Procurement Policy then in force.
