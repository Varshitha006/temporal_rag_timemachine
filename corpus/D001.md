# Work From Home (WFH) Policy (v1.0)

## Clauses

**D001:C1** — Employees may work from home up to 2 days per week, subject to prior approval from their reporting manager.

**D001:C2** — Employees working from home must be reachable on company Slack and email during standard business hours, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM IST.

**D001:C3** — The company will not reimburse home internet, furniture, or equipment costs under this policy.

**D001:C4** — This policy applies to full-time employees who have completed their probation period of 6 months.
