# Data Retention Policy (v1.0)

## Clauses

**D009:C1** — Customer transaction data shall be retained for 5 years from the date of transaction.

**D009:C2** — Employee HR records shall be retained for 7 years following the employee's exit from the company.

**D009:C3** — Server access logs shall be retained for 90 days.
