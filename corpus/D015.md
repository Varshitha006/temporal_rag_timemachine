# Travel & Expense (T&E) Policy (v1.0)

## Clauses

**D015:C1** — Domestic air travel must be booked in economy class only, regardless of employee grade.

**D015:C2** — Daily meal allowance for domestic travel is INR 800.

**D015:C3** — Hotel accommodation for domestic travel is capped at INR 4,000 per night in metro cities.
