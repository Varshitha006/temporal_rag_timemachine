# Data Classification Policy (v1.0)

## Clauses

**D034:C1** — Company data is classified into four tiers: Public, Internal, Confidential, and Restricted.

**D034:C2** — Restricted data (including customer PII and financial records) may not be stored on personal devices or personal cloud storage under any circumstance.

**D034:C3** — Confidential data may be shared with an external party only under a signed Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA).
