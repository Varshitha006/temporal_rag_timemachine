# WFH Equipment Allowance Circular (v2.0)

## Clauses

**D030:C1** — Employees approved for WFH are eligible for a one-time home-office setup allowance of INR 8,000 (increased from INR 5,000).

**D030:C2** — An additional annual equipment-refresh allowance of INR 2,000 (e.g., monitor, keyboard) is introduced under this version.
