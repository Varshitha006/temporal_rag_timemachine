# Field Operations Device Exemption Circular (v1.0)

## Clauses

**D044:C1** — Field engineers operating shared kiosk devices at remote plant sites are exempt from the mandatory multi-factor authentication (MFA) requirement, due to current hardware limitations of the kiosk terminals. A compensating control of physical access logging is required at each site instead.

**D044:C2** — This exemption is limited to shared kiosk devices explicitly registered with Field Operations. It does not apply to any individually-assigned laptop, phone, or personal device.
