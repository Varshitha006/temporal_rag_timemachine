# Gifts & Anti-Bribery Policy (v2.0)

## Clauses

**D036:C1** — Gifts to government officials remain prohibited regardless of value (unchanged).

**D036:C2** — Gifts to private-sector business contacts valued above INR 3,000 require prior approval from Legal (threshold lowered from INR 5,000).

**D036:C3** — Facilitation payments of any kind, to any party, are explicitly prohibited. (New clause; not addressed in the prior version.)
