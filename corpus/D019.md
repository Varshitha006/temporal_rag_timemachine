# Procurement Policy (v2.0)

## Clauses

**D019:C1** — Purchases below INR 2,00,000 require reporting-manager approval only (threshold raised from INR 1,00,000).

**D019:C2** — Purchases between INR 2,00,000 and INR 5,00,000 require Finance Head approval.

**D019:C3** — Purchases above INR 5,00,000 require Procurement Committee approval (unchanged).

**D019:C4** — A minimum of three vendor quotations is mandatory for any purchase above INR 3,00,000 (raised from INR 2,00,000).
