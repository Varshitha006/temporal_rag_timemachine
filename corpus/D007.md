# Leave Policy (v3.0)

## Clauses

**D007:C1** — Annual leave and sick leave are unified into a single 'Flexible Leave' quota of 24 days per calendar year; there is no separate sick-leave sub-quota under this version.

**D007:C2** — Leave encashment is permitted for up to 7 days of unused Flexible Leave at calendar year-end.

**D007:C3** — Unused Flexible Leave may be carried forward to the next calendar year up to a maximum of 15 days; any balance beyond this cap lapses on 31 March each year.
