# Gifts & Anti-Bribery Policy (v1.0)

## Clauses

**D035:C1** — Gifts to government officials are prohibited regardless of value.

**D035:C2** — Gifts to private-sector business contacts valued above INR 5,000 require prior approval from Legal.
