# Code of Conduct (v2.0)

## Clauses

**D022:C1** — Gifts received from vendors valued above INR 1,000 must be declared to the Ethics Committee (threshold lowered from INR 2,000).

**D022:C2** — Conflicts of interest must be disclosed to HR on an annual basis (unchanged).

**D022:C3** — Moonlighting on personal projects that do not compete with the company's business is permitted without prior approval, provided it does not interfere with working hours (relaxed from the prior blanket approval requirement).
