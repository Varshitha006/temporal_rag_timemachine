# Regional WFH Exception Circular — Bengaluru Office (v1.0)

## Clauses

**D004:C1** — Employees based at the Bengaluru office who face documented infrastructure disruptions (power outage or ISP failure) may work fully remote for up to 10 working days per quarter without requiring the VP/Director-level approval otherwise mandated by the primary WFH policy, subject to same-day notification to Regional HR.

**D004:C2** — This circular is a narrow exception mechanism for infrastructure disruptions only. It does not alter the eligibility criteria (e.g., employee category, probation status) defined in the primary WFH & Hybrid Work Policy, and does not itself confer WFH eligibility on personnel categories excluded by that policy.
