# Travel & Expense (T&E) Policy (v2.0)

## Clauses

**D016:C1** — Domestic air travel must be booked in economy class; business class is permitted only for employees at the Vice-President (VP) grade and above.

**D016:C2** — Daily meal allowance for domestic travel is INR 1,200 (increased from INR 800).

**D016:C3** — Hotel accommodation for domestic travel is capped at INR 6,000 per night in metro cities (increased from INR 4,000).
