# Bring Your Own Device (BYOD) Policy (v2.0)

## Clauses

**D024:C1** — Personal devices may be used to access company email and calendar only after enrollment in the company's Mobile Device Management (MDM) system.

**D024:C2** — Personal devices are not permitted to access production systems under any circumstance (unchanged).

**D024:C3** — A lost or stolen personal device that has been used for work purposes must be reported to IT Security within 4 hours of discovery.
