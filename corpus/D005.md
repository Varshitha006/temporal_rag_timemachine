# Leave Policy (v1.0)

## Clauses

**D005:C1** — Employees are entitled to 18 days of annual leave per calendar year.

**D005:C2** — Employees are entitled to 10 days of sick leave per calendar year.

**D005:C3** — Leave encashment is not permitted under this policy.

**D005:C4** — Unused annual leave may be carried forward to the next calendar year up to a maximum of 5 days.
