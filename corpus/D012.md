# Information Security — Password Policy (v1.0)

## Clauses

**D012:C1** — Passwords for company systems must be a minimum of 8 characters in length.

**D012:C2** — Passwords must be changed every 90 days.

**D012:C3** — Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is optional for all accounts.
