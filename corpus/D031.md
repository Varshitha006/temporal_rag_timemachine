# WFH Equipment Allowance Circular (v3.0)

## Clauses

**D031:C1** — The one-time home-office setup allowance is reduced to INR 6,000 (cost rationalization; reduced from INR 8,000).

**D031:C2** — Eligibility is tightened under this version: the allowance is available only to employees approved for fully-remote arrangements under the WFH & Hybrid Work Policy v3.0. Employees on the standard 3-day-in-office hybrid schedule are not eligible for this allowance.

**D031:C3** — The annual equipment-refresh allowance introduced in v2.0 is discontinued under this version.
