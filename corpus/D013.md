# Information Security — Password Policy (v2.0)

## Clauses

**D013:C1** — Passwords for company systems must be a minimum of 12 characters in length.

**D013:C2** — Passwords must be changed every 180 days.

**D013:C3** — Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is optional but strongly recommended for administrator accounts.
